Brown Streaks Inside the stem-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carbendazim

Metalaxyl

Estimated Cost:

Carbendazim:Rs.250-Rs.350 per 100g.

Metalaxyl:Rs.300-rs.450 per 100g.

Dosage:

Carbendazim:2g per liter of water.

Metalaxyl:2g per liter of water.

How to Use:

Take 1liter of clean water in a sprayer or bucket.

Add the recommended fungicide.

Mix well until it dissovles completely.

Pour the solution around the base of the banana plant.

Apply about 1-2 liters solution per plant.

Causes:

Mainly Caused by fungal disease Fusarium Wilt.

Continuous banana cultivation in the same land.

Poor drinage and high soil moisture.

Advantages of Using Pesticides

Stops fungal spread.

Prevents disease spread to nearby plants.

Improves leaf growth.
