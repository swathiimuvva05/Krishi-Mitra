Leaf Spot Disease-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Mancozeb

Estimated Cost:

₹300-₹400 per kg

Dosage:

2-2.5 g per liter of water

How to Use:

Mix Mancozeb in water.

Spray on both sides of leaves.

Repeat spray every 10-15 days.

Causes

Caused by fungal infection called Tikka Leaf Spot.

High humidity and rainy weather.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls fungal infection.

Protects leaves and improves yield.
