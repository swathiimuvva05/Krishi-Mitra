Aphid Attack-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Imidacloprid

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹600-₹900 per liter.

Dosage:

0.5 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix pesticide in water.

Spray on leaves.

Spray in morning or evening.

Causes

Sap-sucking insect Aphid.

Advantages of Pesticides

Kills aphids quickly.

Protects crop yield.
