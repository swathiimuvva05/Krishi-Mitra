Leaves Drying and Hanging-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Mancozeb

Estimated Cost:

Around Rs.300-Rs.400 per kg

Dosage:

Mix 2-2.5 grams per liter of water.

How to Use:

Fill Sprayer with water.

Add Mancozeb and mix well.

Spray on both sides of the banana leaves.

Repeat every 10-15 days if disease continues.

Causes:

Fungas infection due to high humidity and rain.

Poor air circulation between plants.

Advantages of Using Pesticides

Stops fungal spread.

Protects healthy leaves.

Improves leaf growth.
