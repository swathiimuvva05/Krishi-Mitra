Leaves Wilting and Drooping-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carbendazim

Estimated Cost:

Around Rs.250-Rs.350 per 100g.

Dosage:

Mix 2g Carbendazim per 1 litre of water.

How to Use:

Take 1 litre of clean water in a sprayer or bucket.

Add 2grams of Carbendazim.

Mix well until dissolves.

Pour the solution around the base of the banana plant.

Use about 1-2 litres solution per plant.

Causes:

Fungas in soil.

Poor drinage and continuous banana cropping.

Infected planting material.

Advantages of Using Pesticides

Controls fungal infection.

Improves plant survival.

Protects roots and stem.
