Loose Smut-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carboxin

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹400-₹600 per 100 g

Dosage:

2-2.5 g per kg of seed

How to Use:

Treat seeds with fungicide before sowing.

Mix thoroughly and dry in shade.

Then sow treated seeds.

Causes

Fungus called Loose Smut of Wheat.

Advantages of Pesticides

Prevents disease at early stage.

Ensures healthy crop growth.
