Root Rot-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carbendazim

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹250-₹350 per 100 g

Dosage:

2 g per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix Carbendazim in water.

Pour solution around the root zone.

Causes

Soil fungus infection called Groundnut Root Rot.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls root fungus.

Protects plant growth.
