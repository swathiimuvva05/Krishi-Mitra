Thrips Attack-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Dimethoate

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹500-₹700 per liter

Dosage:

2 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix pesticide with water.

Spray on leaves.

Spray during morning or evening.

Causes

Tiny insect called Thrips sucking plant sap.

Advantages of Pesticides

Kills thrips quickly.

Protects leaves and plant growth.
