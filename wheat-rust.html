Rust Disease-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Propiconazole

Estimated Cost

Around ₹800-₹1200 per liter

Dosage:

1 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix fungicide in water.

Spray on wheat leaves uniformly.

Repeat after 15 days if needed.

Causes

Fungal disease called Wheat Rust.

Cool and humid weather conditions.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls rust effectively.

Improves grain yield and quality.
