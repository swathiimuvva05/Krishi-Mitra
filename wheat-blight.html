Leaf Blight-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Mancozeb

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹300-₹400 per kg

Dosage:

2-2.5 g per liter of water

How to Use:

Mix fungicide in water.

Spray on leaves uniformly.

Repeat every 10-15 days.

Causes

Fungal disease called Leaf Blight of Wheat.

High temperature and humidity.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls leaf infection.

Improves yield.
