Stunted Plant growth-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Imidacloprid

Urea

Muriate of Potash(MOP)

Estimated Cost:

Imidacloprid:Rs.600-Rs.900 per liter.

Urea:Rs.6-Rs.7 per kg

Muriate of Potash(MOP):Rs.40-Rs.60 per kg.

Dosage:

Imidacloprid:0.5ml per liter of water.

Urea:50-100g per plant.

Muriate of Potash(MOP):100g per plant.

How to Use:

Imidacloprid:

Take 1liter water in a sprayer.

Add 0.5ml Imidacloprid.

Spray on the top and bottom of leaves.

Spray in morning or evening.

Urea and MOP:

Apply fertilizer around the root zone of the plant.

Mix lightly with soil.

irrigate the field after application.

Causes:

Nutrient deficiency in soil.

Virus infection such as Banana Bunchy Top Virus.

Insect attack like aphids sucking plant sap.

Poor soil fertility and lack of fertilizers.

Water stress or poor irrigation.

Advantages of Using Pesticides

Improves plant growth and height.

Prevents virus spread through insect control.

Increases fruit yield and quality.
