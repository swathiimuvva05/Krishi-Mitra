Termite Attack-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Chlorpyrifos

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹500-₹700 per liter

Dosage:

2.5 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix pesticide in water.

Apply to soil near plant roots.

Causes

Soil pest Termite.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls termites in soil.

Protects roots and plant growth.
