Yellowing of Older Leaves-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Urea

Muriate of Potash(MOP)

Carbendazim

Estimated Cost:

Urea:Rs.6-Rs.7 per kg.

MOP:Rs.40-Rs.60vper kg.

Carbendazim:Rs.250-Rs.350 per 100g.

Dosage:

Urea:50-100g per plant.

Muriate:100g per plant.

Carbendazim:2g per liter of water.

How to Use:

Urea and MOP

Apply fertilizer around the root zone of the banana plant.

Mix lightly with soil.

Water the plant after application.

Carbendazim

Mix 2g Carbendazim in 1 liter water.

Pour the solution arounf the plant roots.

Repeat after 15-20 days if needed.

Causes:

Nutrient deficiency,especially nitrogen or potassium.

Fungal infection such as Fusarium wilt.

Insect attack like aphids sucking plant sap.

Poor soil fertility and lack of fertilizers.

Pest attack like aphids sucking plant sap.

Advantages of Using Pesticides

Restores green healthy leaves.

Improves plant growth and strength.

Prevents disease spread.

Increases banana yield.
