Rust Disease-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Hexaconazole

Estimated Cost:

₹800-₹1200 per liter.

Dosage:

1 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix Hexaconazole in water.

Spray on infected plants.

Repeat after 15 days.

Causes

Caused by fungus Groundnut Rust.

High humidity and rainy weather.

Advantages of Pesticides

Stops rust disease quickly.

Improves plant health.
