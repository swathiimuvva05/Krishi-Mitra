Flag Smut-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carboxin

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹400-₹600 per 100 g

Dosage:

2-2.5 g per kg of seed

How to Use:

Treat seeds before sowing.

Mix fungicide thoroughly with seeds.

Causes

Fungus called Flag Smut of Wheat.

Advantages of Pesticides

Prevents disease from early stage.

Improves plant growth.
