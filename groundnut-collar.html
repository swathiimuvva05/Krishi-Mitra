Collar Rot-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Thiram

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹300-₹500 per kg

Dosage:

3 g per kg of seed (seed treatment).

How to Use:

Mix Thiram with groundnut seeds before sowing.

Coat the seeds evenly.

Dry them in shade before planting.

Causes

Sap-sucking insect called Aphid.

High humidity and rainy weather.

Advantages of Pesticides

Fungal disease called Collar Rot.

Caused by infected soil and high moisture.
