Powdery Mildew-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Sulfur

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹200-₹300 per kg

Dosage:

2-3 g per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix sulfur in water.

Spray on affected leaves.

Repeat every 10-15 days.

Causes

Fungus called Powdery Mildew.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls fungal spread.

Improves leaf health.
