Bud Necrosis Disease-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Imidacloprid

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹600-₹900 per liter.

Dosage:

0.5 ml per liter of water.

How to use:

Mix pesticide with water.

Spray on leaves to control thrips insects.

Causes

Viral disease called Groundnut Bud Necrosis Virus spread by thrips.

Advantages of Pesticides

Controls insects spreading the virus.

Protects crop yield.
