White Grub Attack-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Chlorpyrifos

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹500-₹700 per liter.

Dosage:

2.5 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix pesticide with water.

Apply solution to the soil near plant roots.

Causes

Soil pest called White Grub.

Advantages of Pesticides

Kills soil insects.

Protects plant roots.
