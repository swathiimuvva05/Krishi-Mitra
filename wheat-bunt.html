Karnal Bunt-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Carbendazim

Estimated Cost:

Around ₹250-₹350 per 100 g

Dosage:

2 g per liter of water.

How to Use:

Spray during flowering stage.

Ensure proper coverage of spikes.

Causes

Fungus called Karnal Bunt.

Advantages of Pesticides

Protects grain quality.

Reduces yield loss.
