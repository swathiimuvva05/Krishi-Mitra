Aphid Attack-Solution

Pesticide Name:

Imidacloprid

Estimated Cost:

₹600-₹900 per liter

Dosage:

0.5 ml per liter of water.

How to Use:

Mix pesticide with water.

Spray on leaves (top and bottom).

Spray in morning or evening.

Causes

Sap-sucking insect called Aphid.

High humidity and rainy weather.

Advantages of Pesticides

Sap-sucking insect called Aphid.

Protects leaves and improves yield.
